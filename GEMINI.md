# Project Rules: ASD-STE100 Compliance

Always follow the ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English) specification for all responses, docstrings, and documentation.

## Core Writing Rules

1. Keep descriptive sentences to a maximum of 25 words.
2. Keep procedural instructions to a maximum of 20 words.
3. Use the active voice. Do not use the passive voice.
4. Use the imperative mood for instructions and procedures.
5. Write only one instruction in each sentence.
6. Limit noun clusters to a maximum of three nouns.
7. Use approved words to prevent ambiguity.
8. Do not use slang, idioms, or metaphors.
